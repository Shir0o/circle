# Circle

A personal directory of the people in your circle, centred on keeping track of their birthdays.

## Language

**Person**:
Someone in your circle, identified by name. Only a name is required to create one.
_Avoid_: Contact, member, entry

**Circle**:
The full set of people you have saved.
_Avoid_: Directory, contact list

**Life stage**:
The broad phase a person is in — Kids, Teens, College, or Working. It decides which extra details the app shows for them.
_Avoid_: Age group, category

**Birthday**:
A person's date of birth. It is optional and has three states: a *full birthday* (month, day, and year), a *day-less birthday* (month and year, day unknown), or *no birthday*. A person with no birthday is never shown a fabricated date.
_Avoid_: DOB, date of birth

**Day-less birthday**:
A birthday where the month and year are known but the exact day is not. It is shown as a month and year (`March 1990`), ordered at the start of its month, and never given a day countdown.
_Avoid_: Partial birthday, month-only birthday
